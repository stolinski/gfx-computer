# ADR-0063 — Keyframed Effect parameters: Effect params join the channel vocabulary

## Context

An `effects[]` entry is `{ type, id, params }`. The params are validated once against the Effect's zod schema, and the chain calls `pack(params, ctx)` every frame with the same params. Only `ctx` moves: `progress`, `timestamp`, the canvas size, and the stage scale (`EffectPackContext`, ADR-0012). Nothing in the authored shape can vary over time.

So every Effect that moves today hard-codes its own time law. Water and heatmap multiply `timestamp` by a `speed` param. NTSC and CRT tube run a 30 Hz frame clock. Mask-wipe and particle-dissolve read `progress` directly. Frosted glass grew `growFrom` / `growTo` and `melt.from` / `melt.to`, which is a two-keyframe track only that Effect understands. An author cannot say "pixelSize runs 96 to 1 over 400 ms as the title lands", and an agent cannot either.

ADR-0035 already built the general form: ordered per-channel keyframes, the four-ease enum, welded milliseconds, Cascade welds, DaVinci-style inspector rows, timeline diamonds, and the `motion` operations. Its channel list stopped at Overlay opacity, x, y, scale and rotation, Surface opacity, and the same for Diagram primitives. Effects have no row on the timeline, and their inspector rows are static sliders. The operation inventory splits ownership the same way: `/state/effects/*/params` belongs to `appearance`, and every `animation` pointer belongs to `motion`.

The ruling that shaped this decision, from Scott on 2026-09-14: there is one keyframe system, and effects must not work differently from it.

## Decision

1. **Same block, same rules.** An `effects[]` entry may carry the `animation` block an Overlay carries: `channels` plus an optional `cascade`. Keyframes have the ADR-0035 shape (`atMs`, `value`, `ease`), the ease is the constrained enum, `atMs` is welded milliseconds, and the first keyframe carries no ease. An Effect that declares nothing renders exactly as today.
2. **Channels come from the schema.** Every numeric leaf of the Effect's zod params schema is a channel, addressed by dotted path such as `pixelSize`, `region.x`, or `melt.radius`. A pure walker in `src/lib/utils/` lists the leaves through optional, default, refine, and nested-object wrappers, with each leaf's min, max, and integer flag. `listEffectKeyframeChannels(definition)` in `src/lib/platform/effect-keyframe-channels.ts` is the one list the inspector, the timeline, semantic validation, and the WebMCP vocabulary read. Nothing restates it. Enum, string, color, and boolean leaves are not channels. Integer channels round at sample time.
3. **A definition may only subtract.** `EffectPipelineDefinition.frozenParams` names numeric leaves that never become channels because they seed a fixed-step simulation, a replay, or a hash: every `seed`, and the kernel inputs of fluid ripple, cloth bend, seeded shatter, and tiled deformation. The reason is mechanical: `SeekableSimulationRuntime` continues forward seeks from the current state, so a kernel input that changed mid-run would make a jump-seek and a play-through disagree, which breaks frame determinism. GPU-side evaluation params of those same Effects remain channels.
4. **The static value is the seed.** When a channel exists, the first keyframe seeds the live value, and the static param is the value the Effect returns to when the channel is cleared, exactly as `position.scale` behaves for an Overlay's scale channel. Semantic validation checks every keyframe value against the channel's bounds and rejects an unknown or frozen channel with a path-qualified error.
5. **One evaluator.** `buildCompositionAnimationManifest` emits tweens for every Effect channel into `animState.effectChannels`, keyed by Effect id and channel path, scrubbed by the paused timeline like every other channel. `renderCompositionFrameTo` receives those values in its request and substitutes them into each authored Effect's params before `appendPackChrome`, on the flat, DOF, and stage branches alike. `pack(params, ctx)` and `EffectPackContext` do not change; an Effect renderer never knows whether a param is static or driven. Export scrubs the same manifest, so preview and export agree by construction. There is no second sampler.
6. **Timing.** `atMs` counts from composition start. A `cascade` welds the track's start to an element's enter start or end plus an offset in milliseconds, and `resolveCascadeTimings` carries an `effect:<id>` node whose window is the track's envelope. Effects are cascade subjects, not anchors: `CascadeAnchorSchema` is unchanged, and nothing welds to an Effect in v1.
7. **Never keyframed, and not yet keyframed.** Pack chrome effects are Pack-supplied, not authored, and carry no channels. `depth-of-field` keeps its `pull` ramp and `stage.focus` and `stage.camera` keep their `pull` and `travel` vocabulary, consistent with ADR-0057's ruling that a depth piece makes one decisive move. They are the next consumers, and each arrives behind a piece that needs it.
8. **Frosted glass ramps become sugar.** `growth` (0..1) and `melt.progress` (0..1) become real params the shader reads directly. The existing `growFrom` / `growTo` and `melt.from` / `melt.to` fields stay valid with their defaults and expand into two-keyframe tracks at manifest time, the way `enter` / `exit` expand today. A declared channel takes the pen. No Preset JSON changes; the ramp's curve moves from `smoothstep` to the `smooth` ease, so the affected posters and matrix rows re-derive.
9. **No new inspector section.** The per-channel row of `KeyframesSection.svelte` (value at the playhead, previous, add-or-remove, next, ease-into while parked) is extracted into `KeyframeChannelRow.svelte` with its logic in one module, and every numeric row in an Effect Editor renders through it. A row without a track edits the static param; the diamond starts a track at the playhead; a row with a track edits the keyframe at the playhead. Frozen params render without a diamond. The timeline shows an `effect:<id>` row only once the Effect has a channel or a weld; its diamonds retime and delete like an Overlay's, an unwelded bar is not draggable because an Effect has no clip start, and a welded bar drags its offset.
10. **GUI and agent stay one surface.** The four `motion` keyframe and cascade operations accept `{ kind: 'effect', effectId }`. The `motion` family owns `/state/effects/*/animation`; `appearance` keeps `/state/effects/*/params`, and `appearance.set-effect-params` refuses a write to a channel-owned param and names the `motion` operation, which is what the GUI does by replacing that param's slider with its keyframe row. The vocabulary read lists each Effect's channels with bounds so an agent discovers them rather than guessing.

## Considered options

- **A pure sampler inside the frame renderer** — rejected. It is a second evaluator of the same keyframe grammar, and two implementations drift. The animation manifest is already the deterministic executor for every channel, and the export path scrubs it.
- **Per-Effect named animation params, the frosted-glass way** — rejected, as ADR-0035 §2 already rejected pipeline-exposed named params. That path is exactly what this decision retires.
- **A restated keyframeable list per Effect** — rejected. The schema already knows which leaves are numbers and what their bounds are; a definition only subtracts, through `frozenParams`. Deriving the vocabulary from the live schema is the same rule ADR-0054 applies to enums.
- **Effects as cascade anchors** — deferred. An Effect has no entrance, and no piece has asked to weld to one.
- **Keyframing `depth-of-field`, the Stage focus, and the camera** — deferred, to stay inside ADR-0057's one-move ruling until a piece needs more.
- **Top-level numeric params only** — rejected. A lens region sliding across the frame is the first thing an author reaches for, and the dotted path costs nothing more.
- **Generating every Effect Editor from the schema** — rejected as an incidental refactor. Only the numeric rows change, to the shared row.
- **A curve editor or bezier values** — rejected. The constrained ease enum is the taste guardrail ADR-0035 set, and GFX is not a node compositor.

## Consequences

- `engine-schema.ts` gains the Effect `animation` block and effect-aware cycle validation; `definition-types.ts` gains `frozenParams`; `effect-keyframe-channels.ts` and a schema walker in `src/lib/utils/` list the channels; `cascade-timing.ts`, `composition-animation-manifest.ts`, `anim-state.svelte.ts`, and `composition-frame-renderer.ts` carry the values; `composition-keyframe-cascade-operations.ts`, `webmcp-motion-tools.ts`, `webmcp-operation-inventory.ts`, and `webmcp-tool-preconditions.ts` expose the subject; `composition-timeline-tracks.ts` and `timeline-entity-identity.ts` add the row; `KeyframeChannelRow.svelte`, `EffectParamRow.svelte`, and every Effect Editor's numeric rows change; frosted glass gains `growth` and `melt.progress`.
- `docs/preset-format.md`, `docs/engine-architecture.md`, `docs/CONTEXT.md`, and `docs/preset-format.schema.json` update inside the leaves that change what they describe.
- The fixture `effect-keyframes-demo` proves the feature end to end: a pixelation resolve welded to a title landing and a lens sliding across the frame, in both orientations under every Pack, with a committed poster and a render-matrix lane.
- The linter's entrance rules (G6, A1, A2, L4) do not apply to Effect channels, because an Effect track is not an entrance. Effects emit no sound cues.
- Simulation Effects keep their kernels pure: only their GPU-side params are channels.
- Delivery is direct, in a linked worktree, gated by `pnpm check`, `pnpm test`, `pnpm verify-presets`, and the affected render-matrix lanes. Scott looks at the visual leaves on gfx.robo.online after integration or on a preview when he asks; no factory run and no gfx-review gate packet.
